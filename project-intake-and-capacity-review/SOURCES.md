# Sources and credits

Written by Fully Powered AI in its own words. One published open-source method shaped it: the senior project manager guide in alirezarezvani/claude-skills (MIT), and its companion reference on prioritization models, both under project-management/skills/senior-pm/. From them comes the idea of ranking every initiative by a weighted score across several criteria so that a new proposal can be set against the work already running, and the idea of holding a team's load inside a sustainable range with room to spare, instead of planning it to full. Fully Powered AI wrote the rest: scoring on the business's own criteria with a written meaning for each score and a quotation as evidence for every proposed score, the monthly load by role with and without the proposal, the headroom under the ceiling, the list of committed work that would have to give way (lowest score first, with fixed work kept apart), the month-by-month test of whether the proposal would fit if it started later, the stops when headings or totals do not match, and the split of decisions between the template and the portfolio owner. No third-party text is reused.
