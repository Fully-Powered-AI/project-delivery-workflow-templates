# Fully Powered Capability Templates: Project and Delivery

Projects that finish the way they were promised. These capability templates take the scoping, tracking and handover work that keeps a project honest and give an AI assistant a precise way to prepare it from the plan and the notes, with a person making every call on scope, date and cost.

This repository holds 7 capability templates for project managers, delivery leads and the people who sponsor a project. Each one is a single piece of work with its own folder: what it is for and who starts it, the complete template an AI assistant follows, a worked example with invented data, what to change for your company, what has been checked, and its sources.

## The capabilities

- **[Project handover readiness check](project-handover-readiness-check/)**: When a finished deliverable is ready for the team that will run it, the project manager brings one checklist against that team's own requirements: acceptance evidence, instructions, access, training, support owners and open items. The receiving owner accepts or names the conditions still to meet.
- **[Project intake and capacity review](project-intake-and-capacity-review/)**: When a new initiative is proposed, the portfolio owner sees it scored on the business's own criteria beside the projects already committed, the delivery days it would use month by month, and what it would crowd out or push later. The owner accepts, defers or rejects it.
- **[Project lessons review](project-lessons-review/)**: When a phase or a project ends, the project manager gets one blameless review: whether it achieved what it was for, how scope, schedule and budget moved and why, whether the success measures were the right ones, and practice changes for the team to choose, each with an owner and a follow-up date.
- **[Project scope baseline](project-scope-baseline/)**: A project manager turns an approved brief into a draft scope baseline: the deliverables, what is excluded, the acceptance criteria for each deliverable, the constraints and the open questions for the sponsor. Nothing implied counts as scope until the sponsor says so.
- **[Schedule recovery options](schedule-recovery-options/)**: When a committed milestone slips, the project manager brings the sponsor two to four ways back: what each changes, the dates it gives every later milestone and dependency, and the assumptions it rests on. The sponsor chooses; delivery leads confirm the dates.
- **[Scope change impact review](scope-change-impact-review/)**: When new work turns up in a project plan, the project manager gets one review: every deliverable compared with the agreed baseline, each addition marked approved or not, its estimated effect on effort, cost and the end date, and the decision the sponsor has to make. The sponsor accepts or declines each one.
- **[Stakeholder and decision rights map](stakeholder-and-decision-rights-map/)**: Before kickoff, the project manager holds one map of everyone the project touches: each group placed by influence and interest, every key decision with exactly one accountable owner, the decision makers still missing, and who hears what, when and from whom. The sponsor confirms every decision right.

## How to use one

Open a folder and start with its README. Every folder stands alone, so copy the one you want and leave the rest. The templates are written for ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot and Gemini: paste the instruction block and the output format in as custom instructions, or load the folder in a product that reads `SKILL.md` packages.

In every template a person starts the work, supplies the inputs and decides. The template prepares and checks. Anything that sends, posts, pays or changes a record waits for a person to approve it.

## What SKILL.md is

Each folder holds a file named `SKILL.md`. It is the technical file: the same instructions and output format as the template, in the packaged form that AI products which load instructions from a folder read. It is generated from `TEMPLATE.md`, so the two always agree. The capability template is the whole folder; `SKILL.md` is one way of loading it. If your AI product does not read that format, use `TEMPLATE.md`.

## What "checked" means here

Each folder says exactly what was checked and when: who wrote it, that its sources and rights were reviewed, the date of its editorial review, and whether it has been tried on its invented example in a named AI product. Nothing is described as tested for a client unless it was. Test a template on your own past work before your team relies on it; each one comes with acceptance tests for that.
