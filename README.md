**Quick start**

1. **Turn your team's updates into a status report.** Open ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot or Gemini. Copy the text below into a new chat, replace the square brackets with what you have, and send it. Use the updates your team already wrote this week. Nothing needs to be downloaded or signed up for.

   ```text
   You are helping me draft a status report for one project and one period.
   I will read it, confirm every colour and decide what to send. You
   prepare; I decide. Do not send anything, mark anything done or change
   any plan. Here is my milestone plan: [paste your milestones, owners, due
   dates and the evidence needed]. Here are this period's updates from each
   workstream lead: [paste them]. Here is last period's report: [paste it].
   My notes: [paste anything the sponsor decided]. Write one page for the
   sponsor: an overall red, amber or green with one sentence of reason;
   decisions needed and by when; dates at risk; each workstream with its
   reason; and open blockers. Call a milestone done only if the evidence is
   there. Then list, for me only, the statuses to confirm and the questions
   you could not answer.
   ```

2. Read what comes back. It marks what it checked and what is left for you. What comes back, with invented data:

   ```text
   PROJECT STATUS REPORT: New project-accounting system, 9 to 13 November 2026
   DRAFT for the Operations manager's confirmation. Not for sending until every colour is confirmed.

   For: Managing director. Report date: Friday 13 November 2026. Next report: Friday 20 November 2026. End date: Monday 4 January 2027.
   Prepared from: the milestone plan (12 milestones, 4 workstreams); updates from 4 of 4 workstreams; the last report of 6 November 2026; the project lead's notes; default rules.

   OVERALL: RED (was AMBER). Red because the trial load of open projects (A2) is past due and not yet complete, and if it finishes on 18 November the reporting milestones are forecast for December; go-live on 4 January 2027 still holds, and you have two decisions to make, by 18 and 20 November.

   DECISIONS NEEDED FROM THE SPONSOR: 2
   ```

3. Stop there. You will know if this is for you. ## The project workflow templates here today

- **[Project portfolio status roll-up](portfolio-status-roll-up/)**: The head of delivery takes one page to leadership each fortnight or month, built from the reports project leads already write: every project red, amber or green as its own lead stated it, what moved, the decisions leadership is asked for, and where two reports contradict each other or a status has no reason.
- **[Project handover readiness check](project-handover-readiness-check/)**: When a finished deliverable is ready for the team that will run it, the project manager brings one checklist against that team's own requirements: acceptance evidence, instructions, access, training, support owners and open items. The receiving owner accepts or names the conditions still to meet.
- **[Project intake and capacity review](project-intake-and-capacity-review/)**: When a new initiative is proposed, the portfolio owner sees it scored on the business's own criteria beside the projects already committed, the delivery days it would use month by month, and what it would crowd out or push later. The owner accepts, defers or rejects it.
- **[Project lessons review](project-lessons-review/)**: When a phase or a project ends, the project manager gets one blameless review: whether it achieved what it was for, how scope, schedule and budget moved and why, whether the success measures were the right ones, and practice changes for the team to choose, each with an owner and a follow-up date.
- **[Project scope baseline](project-scope-baseline/)**: A project manager turns an approved brief into a draft scope baseline: the deliverables, what is excluded, the acceptance criteria for each deliverable, the constraints and the open questions for the sponsor. Nothing implied counts as scope until the sponsor says so.
- **[Project status report](project-status-report/)**: The lead of an internal project gets a status report the sponsor can read in two minutes: each workstream red, amber or green with the reason, what moved, who must clear each blocker, the decisions needed and the dates at risk. No milestone is called done without evidence, and the lead confirms every status.
- **[Schedule recovery options](schedule-recovery-options/)**: When a committed milestone slips, the project manager brings the sponsor two to four ways back: what each changes, the dates it gives every later milestone and dependency, and the assumptions it rests on. The sponsor chooses; delivery leads confirm the dates.
- **[Scope change impact review](scope-change-impact-review/)**: When new work turns up in a project plan, the project manager gets one review: every deliverable compared with the agreed baseline, each addition marked approved or not, its estimated effect on effort, cost and the end date, and the decision the sponsor has to make. The sponsor accepts or declines each one.
- **[Sprint plan against capacity](sprint-capacity-plan/)**: Before each planning meeting, the delivery lead or scrum master brings a draft plan for the next sprint: the days each person really has, a forecast from the team's own recent delivery, the carryover, the ranked backlog cut at that figure, and what each item waits on. The team decides the commitment.
- **[Stakeholder and decision rights map](stakeholder-and-decision-rights-map/)**: Before kickoff, the project manager holds one map of everyone the project touches: each group placed by influence and interest, every key decision with exactly one accountable owner, the decision makers still missing, and who hears what, when and from whom. The sponsor confirms every decision right.

## How to use one

Open a folder and start with its README. Every folder stands alone, so copy the one you want and leave the rest. The templates are written for ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot and Gemini: paste the instruction block and the output format in as custom instructions, or load the folder in a product that reads `SKILL.md` packages.

To take the whole collection, press **Use this template** at the top of this page: it copies every folder into a repository in your own account, yours to change.

In every template a person starts the work, supplies the inputs and decides. The template prepares and checks. Anything that sends, posts, pays or changes a record waits for a person to approve it.

## What SKILL.md is

Each folder holds a file named `SKILL.md`. It is the technical file: the same instructions and output format as the template, in the packaged form that AI products which load instructions from a folder read. It is generated from `TEMPLATE.md`, so the two always agree. The workflow template is the whole folder; `SKILL.md` is one way of loading it. If your AI product does not read that format, use `TEMPLATE.md`.

## What "checked" means here

Each folder says exactly what was checked and when: who wrote it, that its sources and rights were reviewed, the date of its editorial review, and whether it has been tried on its invented example in a named AI product. Nothing is described as tested for a client unless it was. Test a template on your own past work before your team relies on it; each one comes with acceptance tests for that.
