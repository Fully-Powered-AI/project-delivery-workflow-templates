Written by Fully Powered AI in its own words. One published open-source method shaped it: the stakeholder map in qa-aman/claude-skills (MIT), from its main instructions file and its reference file on how to engage each group on the grid. From it come sweeping a wide range of affected groups before mapping anyone; a two-by-two grid of influence against interest; a decision chart giving each key decision one person who answers for it, with the other roles marked as doing the work, asked first or told after, and a decision without one clear owner is a problem to settle before work starts; a contact plan for each group whose rhythm and channel follow its place on the grid; and a fresh look at the map when a phase ends or someone's role changes. Fully Powered AI added the default column headings and the stop when a list does not match them; placement rules tied to what each group controls and how much its work changes, with the project manager's own placement kept and any difference shown; eight coverage questions; the check against five decisions every project needs; the check of each accountable owner against the date the decision is due; consult-by and tell-by dates counted in working days; a named messenger for every group; recording only concerns people have actually raised and leaving out remarks about individuals; and the sponsor's confirmation of every decision right. No third-party text is reused.
