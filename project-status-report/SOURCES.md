# Sources and credits

Written by Fully Powered AI in its own words. Two published open-source methods shaped it. From the progress tracker in panaversity/agentfactory-business-plugins (Apache-2.0): a red, amber or green mark for each project with a short reason; the three tests behind each colour (a milestone missed or at risk, a blocker, and a sponsor decision overdue or due soon); the split between a blocker that has a workaround and one that has none; refusing a green mark after a missed date unless someone agreed to the later date; a named person to clear every blocker, a count of the days each blocker has been open, and raising a blocker that has sat for over a week; milestones with an owner, a stated finish, what each depends on and what shows it is done; and keeping the whole report to one page with the overall mark at the top. From the stakeholder communication guide in rampstackco/claude-skills (MIT): giving the reader the conclusion in the first sentence, lifting each request for a decision out of the status with its options, a recommendation and a date, reporting results rather than effort, treating a status that stays amber as a warning sign, chasing a request that got no answer, and naming the date of the next report. Fully Powered AI added the check of every completed milestone against the evidence the plan asks for; colours tied to the plan's dates and that evidence, with a fourth red test for an end date forecast to move, and no colour greener than either the rules or the workstream lead's own proposal; the dates at risk traced through what each milestone waits on; the comparison with the last report; the stop when an export's headings do not match; and the project lead's confirmation of every mark before anything goes. No third-party text is reused.
