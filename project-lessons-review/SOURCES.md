# Sources and credits

Written by Fully Powered AI in its own words. One published open-source method shaped it: the product retrospective in panaversity/agentfactory-business-plugins (Apache-2.0). From it come the idea of looking back on finished work through four lenses (whether it achieved its purpose, how its delivery went against the plan, whether its success measures were sound, and what the team would do another way next time); weighing each measure on whether data for it existed, whether it responded to the work, whether it reflected the purpose and whether a level counting as a miss had been fixed in advance; holding back the judgement on results while they are not in yet and still looking at delivery; a mark for each measure and one overall verdict that allows for results not yet in, with the rules for assigning them left to Fully Powered AI; giving the things that worked the same attention as the problems; turning each lesson into a concrete step that can be checked rather than a general resolve; and giving each follow-on action an owner and a date. Fully Powered AI added the budget, and the tracing of every scope, schedule and cost movement to a recorded change, split into approved, recorded without approval and untraced; the fixed exports with default headings and the stop when one does not match; the rules for assigning the marks and the verdict; the check on the changes chosen at the last review; the blameless rules, which leave out remarks about a person and keep only the step they point to; the rule that the project manager and delivery leads choose every change and that no owner or date is invented; and the line between this review and incident investigation, launch checks and win-loss reviews. No third-party text is reused.
